Older Puppies & Adult Dogs

From time to time we have older puppies or adult dogs looking for exceptional homes. They're a wonderful option for anyone who would love a Havanese but would prefer to skip the earliest puppy stages. Availability varies, so if this sounds like you, please get in touch and we'll let you know when someone suitable comes along.

Retired Breeding Females

Our girls sometimes finish their breeding careers and are ready to enjoy life as a much-loved family companion. We'd really like to place them in excellent homes, and they have a lot to offer:

  • Rigorously health tested, with results you can see for yourself.
  • Well socialised, having grown up as part of our household.
  • Settled and past the puppy stage, ideal if you'd rather not go through house training and teething.

Show Prospects Who Didn't Quite Make It

We sometimes keep puppies on for a while to take a closer look at whether they'll suit the show ring. Occasionally we find a very small detail that isn't quite right for showing, such as a bite that isn't correct or a tail that isn't carried as the standard asks.

Otherwise they're perfectly healthy puppies from rigorously health-checked parents, fully vaccinated and ready to go. They're usually between six months and a year old, and sometimes a little younger.
