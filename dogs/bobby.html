Bobbi

AM CH That's Boy Wonder at Lake Michigan

Born: 2019  |  Leased from David & Darlene Skiris

Bobbi is our leased American Champion stud dog, bringing exceptional type, coat, and showmanship to the Alphapups breeding programme. An American Champion with a proven show record in the USA, Bobbi is a striking black and white parti Havanese with outstanding conformation and a wonderful temperament.

His influence on our breeding programme has been immediate and significant — his litter with Poppy in January 2026 produced eight beautiful puppies, demonstrating both his fertility and his ability to pass on his exceptional qualities. We are delighted to have him at Alphapups and look forward to seeing his offspring develop.

Bobbi is CHIC certified (#211427) and thoroughly health tested: hips OFA scored Good, elbows and patella Normal, eyes clear (2025), heart Normal/Clear, and full adult dentition confirmed. His pedigree combines some of the finest American Havanese bloodlines.

Embark DNA testing: clear for all 269 genetic health conditions tested.
