Baby

Katie's Sister  |  Breeding Female

Baby is Katie's sister and a key part of the Alphapups breeding programme. A striking chocolate and tan Havanese with a wonderful temperament, Baby brings exceptional type and a rich, dark coat to our litters.

Baby has had a successful show career in her own right and her puppies are consistently of excellent quality. Her show wins include placements at Championship level, and she has qualified for Crufts on multiple occasions.

Baby is fully health tested: her hips are OFA scored Excellent and her elbows Normal, her heart tested clear with no murmur detected, and her BVA/KC eye exam found no abnormalities. Her patella scored Grade 0 (right) and a mild Grade 1 (left) — within normal limits but monitored as part of her ongoing care. Her combination of exceptional coat, type and character makes her an invaluable part of the Alphapups kennel.

Embark DNA testing: clear for all but two results — one copy of the FGF4 variant associated with Type I IVDD (intervertebral disc disease) risk — a risk marker rather than a diagnosis, and its predictive value is debated within the breed — and one copy of the ALT activity variant. ALT activity is flagged by Embark as a "notable" result rather than a disease risk.
