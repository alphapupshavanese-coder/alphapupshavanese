Sydney

AM CH That's Eh Coat of Many Colours at Alphapups

Born: 5th April 2021 (Imported from the USA)  |  Breeding Female

Sydney is an American Champion Havanese with a beautiful gold sable and white coat, imported to Alphapups from the United States. Bred by C & J Stutts and D & D Scheiris, she is sired by AM CH That's a Tribute to a Truly Great Man out of AM CH That's a Sip of Southern Sweet Tea, and is jointly owned by Evan Woolcock and David & Darlene Scheiris.

Sydney brings exceptional American bloodlines and type into our breeding programme, and her striking colouring and lovely temperament make her a wonderful addition to the Alphapups girls.

Sydney is extensively health tested and holds a CHIC number (Canine Health Information Center), with clearances for hips (Good), elbows (Normal), patella (Normal), eyes (Normal, 2025), Legg-Calve-Perthes (Normal), and basic cardiac (Normal/Clear), along with a BAER hearing test confirming no evidence of congenital deafness.
