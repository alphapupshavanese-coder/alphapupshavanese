Contact

We'd love to hear from you — whether you're enquiring about our waiting list, have a question about the breed, or would like to know more about one of our dogs.

We're based in North Somerset, not far from Bristol, UK. As a small home breeder, we don't publish our full address online — once you're on our waiting list, or have spoken with us by phone or video call, we're always happy to arrange a visit.

The best way to reach us is by email, and we aim to reply as quickly as we can.
